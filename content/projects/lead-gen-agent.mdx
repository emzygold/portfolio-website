## Overview

The Lead Generation Agent is an intelligent system designed to automate the tedious process of finding and qualifying potential clients. Built with Python and AI, it scans multiple platforms, evaluates leads based on custom criteria, and delivers a curated list of high-quality prospects.

## The Problem

As a freelancer, finding clients is a constant challenge. Manually searching through job boards, social media, and directories takes hours every week — time that could be spent on actual client work.

## The Solution

I built an automated agent that:

- **Scans multiple sources** — job boards, social platforms, and business directories
- **Qualifies leads** — uses AI to score and rank leads based on relevance, budget, and project fit
- **Organizes results** — delivers structured data with contact information and project details
- **Runs on schedule** — automated daily runs with email notifications for new high-quality leads

## Tech Stack

- **Python** — core logic and orchestration
- **AI/ML** — natural language processing for lead qualification
- **Automation** — scheduled tasks, API integrations
- **Data Pipeline** — structured data extraction and storage

## Key Features

1. **Multi-source scanning** — aggregates leads from various platforms
2. **AI-powered scoring** — intelligent ranking based on project requirements
3. **Automated outreach** — templated responses for qualified leads
4. **Dashboard** — visual overview of lead pipeline and conversion rates

## Results

- Reduced lead research time by **80%**
- Increased qualified lead discovery by **3x**
- Automated follow-up for **100%** of qualified leads
