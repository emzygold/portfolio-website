## Overview

An AI-powered customer support chatbot that understands natural language, provides instant answers to common questions, and seamlessly escalates complex issues to human support agents.

## The Problem

A client's support team was overwhelmed with repetitive questions — order status, refund policies, shipping times. Agents spent 70% of their time answering the same questions instead of handling complex cases.

## The Solution

I built an AI chatbot that:

- **Understands intent** — uses NLP to understand what customers are really asking
- **Answers instantly** — responds to FAQs with accurate, contextual answers
- **Learns over time** — improves responses based on feedback
- **Escalates smartly** — routes complex issues to the right human agent with full context
- **Works 24/7** — handles inquiries outside business hours

## Tech Stack

- **Python** — NLP engine and API backend
- **React** — chat widget frontend
- **AI/NLP** — intent classification and entity extraction
- **WebSockets** — real-time messaging
- **PostgreSQL** — conversation history and analytics

## Key Features

1. **Natural language understanding** — handles typos, slang, and varied phrasing
2. **Context awareness** — remembers conversation history
3. **Multi-language** — supports English and basic Spanish
4. **Analytics dashboard** — track resolution rates, common topics, satisfaction
5. **Easy customization** — update knowledge base without coding
6. **Widget embed** — drop into any website with one script tag

## Results

- Resolved **65% of inquiries** without human intervention
- Reduced average response time from **4 hours to 30 seconds**
- Customer satisfaction score improved by **22%**
- Support team could focus on high-value, complex cases
