## Overview

A complete project management system built around Trello, designed to bring order to chaotic workflows. This setup transforms how teams plan, track, and deliver projects — from initial briefing to final handoff.

## The Problem

The client's team was drowning in scattered tasks. Work lived in emails, Slack threads, sticky notes, and spreadsheets. No one had a clear picture of what was in progress, what was blocked, or what was coming next. Deadlines were missed regularly.

## The Solution

I designed and implemented a structured Trello-based project management system that includes:

- **Custom board templates** — pre-built for different project types (client work, internal, sprints)
- **Kanban workflow** — clear stages: Backlog → To Do → In Progress → Review → Done
- **Card templates** — standardized task cards with checklists, due dates, and labels
- **Automation rules** — Butler automations for recurring tasks, notifications, and status updates
- **Power-Up integrations** — connected to Slack, Google Drive, and time tracking

## Key Features

1. **Board templates** — spin up new projects in minutes with pre-configured workflows
2. **Color-coded labels** — priority levels (urgent, high, medium, low) and categories
3. **Custom fields** — track estimated hours, actual hours, and project health
4. **Automated notifications** — team gets alerted when tasks are assigned or due dates approach
5. **Weekly dashboards** — overview cards summarizing progress, blockers, and upcoming deadlines
6. **Sprint planning** — dedicated sprint boards with story points and velocity tracking

## Tools & Integrations

- **Trello** — core kanban board and task management
- **Butler** — Trello's built-in automation for rules, buttons, and scheduled commands
- **Slack** — real-time notifications and updates
- **Google Drive** — file attachments and document linking
- **Calendar Power-Up** — visual timeline of deadlines

## Results

- **40% fewer missed deadlines** — clear visibility into what's due when
- **2 hours/week saved** per team member on status updates and check-ins
- **Onboarding time cut in half** — new team members get productive faster with templates
- Team reported feeling **significantly less stressed** about project tracking
