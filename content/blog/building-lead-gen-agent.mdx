## The Problem

Finding clients as a freelancer is exhausting. You spend hours scrolling through job boards, evaluating opportunities, and crafting proposals — often for jobs that aren't even a good fit. I knew there had to be a better way.

## The Idea

What if an AI agent could do the searching for me? Not just scraping job listings, but actually **understanding** what makes a good lead based on my skills and preferences.

## Building the Agent

### Step 1: Define What a Good Lead Looks Like

First, I created a scoring system based on:
- **Relevance** — does the project match my skills?
- **Budget** — is the budget realistic?
- **Timeline** — can I deliver in the timeframe?
- **Client quality** — what's their hiring history?

### Step 2: Data Collection

The agent scans multiple sources on a schedule, collecting raw opportunity data and normalizing it into a consistent format.

### Step 3: AI Scoring

Each lead gets scored by an AI model that considers all the factors above. The model was fine-tuned on my past successful projects to understand what "good" looks like for me specifically.

### Step 4: Notification & Action

High-scoring leads trigger notifications with a summary and suggested action. The system can even draft initial outreach messages.

## Results

After running for a month:
- **80% less time** spent on lead research
- **3x more qualified leads** discovered
- **Higher conversion rate** because I'm only reaching out to great-fit opportunities

## Lessons Learned

1. **Start simple** — my first version was just keyword matching, and it was still useful
2. **Iterate on the scoring** — the AI model improved dramatically with feedback
3. **Automation compounds** — small time savings add up to huge productivity gains

## What's Next

I'm planning to add automated proposal generation and A/B testing different outreach strategies. The goal is to make the entire client acquisition pipeline as hands-off as possible.
